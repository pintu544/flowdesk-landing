Features

Everything a small support team needs

No bloated enterprise modules. Just the tools that help a team of 2–20 answer customers well.

One shared inbox

Email, live chat and social messages arrive in a single queue, so nothing slips through the cracks between tools.

Collision-free teamwork

See who is viewing and replying to a ticket in real time. Assign owners and hand off with private notes, not forwarded emails.

Automations that save hours

Auto-tag, route and prioritise tickets with simple rules. Saved replies answer the common questions in two clicks.

Reports you can act on

First-response time, resolution time and CSAT at a glance. Spot the busy hours and staff them before customers wait.

A help centre that deflects tickets

Publish answers to common questions on your own help site. Customers help themselves; your inbox stays quiet.

Private by default

Role-based access, audit logs and data export whenever you want it. Your customers' conversations stay yours.

How it works

From scattered messages to a calm queue in three steps

1

Connect your channels

Forward your support email and plug in the website chat widget. Every message starts landing in one place within minutes.

2

Work the queue together

Tickets are assigned, prioritised and answered as a team — with full history on every customer, right beside the reply box.

3

Improve every week

Weekly reports show what slowed you down. Add a help-centre article or an automation, and watch the queue shrink.
